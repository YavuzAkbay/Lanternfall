# How Lanternfall is developed

Lanternfall is a personal passion project that I chose to share publicly. It grows from the fullscreen Playnite experience I want for myself, plus a controller-only Browser and YouTube, and I want to keep enjoying both using it and working on it.

Feedback, bug reports, and ideas are welcome. Sharing an idea doesn't create a commitment to implement it, and popularity alone doesn't decide the direction of the project. Lanternfall will grow when I have the time and motivation, at a pace that keeps it enjoyable.

## Priorities

- **Bugs and stability first.** I aim to investigate reproducible issues and compatibility problems as time allows. Reports with steps to reproduce, logs, and system details are especially helpful. Reporting a bug doesn't guarantee an immediate fix.
- **Polish matters.** Performance, consistency, accessibility, and refining existing features are worthwhile updates even when nothing new is added.

Ideas live in [Discussions > Ideas](https://github.com/YavuzAkbay/Lanternfall/discussions/categories/ideas).
