The color themes in `theme/Palettes` are generated from the palettes of [Omarchy](https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy)'s

## Ideas from other add-ons
Handing the screen to a game after the loading screen (check it is really in front, retry, and a short watch for launchers that
pull Playnite forward again) follows the approach of Aniki Helper's game launch splash by Mike Aniki
(https://github.com/Mike-Aniki/AnikiHelper, MIT License). The code is Selene's own.

## Add-ons installed by the setup
Selene's first-launch setup installs these Playnite add-ons through Playnite's own installer; they are not bundled and
keep their own licenses: Extra Metadata Loader (darklinkpower), Ludusavi (mtkennerly), HowLongToBeat and GameActivity
(Lacro59), Playnite Achievements (justin-delano), DuplicateHider (felixkmh), UniPlaySong (aHuddini) and Audio Switcher
(Naerian). For save backups it also downloads the [Ludusavi](https://github.com/mtkennerly/ludusavi) program
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020 Matthew T. Kennerly) into Selene Core's data folder.

## Preview sample art
`theme/Media/Sample` holds pictures drawn by `tools/make_lanternfall_media.ps1`, used only to show what a home looks like in the
setup's previews before any of your own games are in. When you allow downloads, the setup can fetch Steam's art for the example game
onto your own computer; nothing of it is shipped here.

## Wallpapers
`theme/Wallpapers` holds public-domain paintings (Gruvbox and Kanagawa themes) and soft wallpapers drawn by
`tools/make_lanternfall_media.ps1` (every other theme):
- "The Great Wave off Kanagawa" by Katsushika Hokusai (public domain) - `kanagawa/1-kanagawa.jpg`
- "The Backwater" by Charles William Wyllie (public domain) - `gruvbox/1-the-backwater.jpg`

The paintings came by way of [Omarchy](https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy)'s theme collection.
